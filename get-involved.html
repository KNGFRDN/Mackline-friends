Lipa na M-Pesa
Donate via Paybill
  1. Open the M-Pesa menu on your phone.
  2. Select Lipa na M-Pesa, then Pay Bill.
  3. Enter the Paybill number: To be updated.
  4. Enter the account number: Mackline & Friends.
  5. Enter your donation amount, confirm the details, and enter your M-Pesa PIN.
